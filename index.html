A co-op arcade shooter
Breakout
from Russia

Lured to Kazakhstan with the promise of a factory job, you wake up in a Russian staging camp in occupied Ukraine. With a squad of fellow African conscripts, you fight your way through the camp, a convoy route and a cratered border highway — toward the Ukrainian line, to surrender.

Plays with keyboard & mouse, a controller (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro), or touch on phones and tablets. Single player: three AI brothers fight with you. Local co-op: plug in up to three gamepads for 2–4 player split-screen — each human replaces an AI squadmate. A work of fiction, inspired by reports of foreign workers coerced into military service.

Breakout Stages

Squad & Co-op

Player 1 uses keyboard & mouse. Press A or Start on a gamepad to join as Player 2–4; press B to leave. Empty slots are filled by AI squadmates.

Menus work with a controller too: D-pad or stick to move, A to select, B to go back. Rolling makes you briefly immune to gunfire (not explosions). Aim assist and auto-fire can be changed in Settings.

Squad Momentum fills from kills — chain kills within a couple of seconds for big bonuses. Revives and objectives add momentum too. Scavenged optics, drum magazines and suppressors go into the squad stash; snap them on at any field workbench.
